KnuPdf icons (knupdf.svg, knupdf-small.svg, knupdf-document.svg and every file rendered from them
in this directory: *.png, *.ico).

Original artwork drawn for KnuPdf from simple geometric shapes. The "ㅋ" is the outline of the
NanumSquare Bold glyph (NAVER, SIL Open Font License 1.1), converted to a plain path; the OFL
allows using a font's glyphs in artwork, and no font software is included. The "PDF" label on the
document icon is set in DejaVu Sans when rendered. The colours follow the KNUPDF screen design
(Kangwon blue #0A4A9F, navy #0B1F3F). Licensed like the rest of KnuPdf, under either of the MIT
licence or the Apache License 2.0, at your option.
